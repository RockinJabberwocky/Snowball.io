🚜 Snowball.io 3D Battle Arena

A fast-paced 3D WebGL battle royale browser game built with Three.js and the Web Audio API. Drive a ski resort snowcat (snow groomer), roll giant snowballs against your front plow blade, and blast opponents off a shrinking iceberg into the icy sea!

🎮 Gameplay Overview

In Snowball.io 3D, you pilot a ski area snowcat on a floating circular iceberg. As you move across the ice, a snowball accumulates on your vehicle's front plow blade:

Roll & Grow: The longer you drive, the larger your snowball becomes.

Launch & Knockback: Launch your snowball at opponents. Larger snowballs deliver heavier knockback, dynamic low-pitched impact thuds, and area-of-effect (AOE) shockwaves.

Shrinking Arena: The iceberg constantly shrinks over time, forcing remaining players into close-quarters combat.

Elimination & Leveling: Knock opponents off the edge into the ocean to gain kills, level up, increase your top speed, and scale up your snowcat!

✨ Features & Highlights

Single-File Architecture: Pure HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Three.js self-contained in a single lightweight file.

Procedural Sound Engine: Web Audio API synthesizes custom SFX for engine hums, snowball rolling, launches, heavy impacts, water splashes, and victory fanfares without external audio files.

Advanced AI Bot Logic:

1v1 Duel Distribution: Bots claim individual targets across the arena to prevent crowding and dogpiling on the player.

Tactical Navigation: Bots maintain personal spacing, flank around targets, and avoid iceberg edges.

Dynamic Physics & Visual FX:

Exponential knockback scale based on snowball volume.

Particle systems for snow explosions and ocean splashes.

Real-time minimap and leaderboard HUD.
